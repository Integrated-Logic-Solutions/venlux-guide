Venlux lighting control · for integrators and installers

The screens you'll use on a Venlux job

Venlux runs on Crestron 4-Series processors and controls Zūm wired lighting plus DMX through Pharos. The site is described in Venlux Setup, a desktop app, and deployed to the processors. On site, one app serves two kinds of touch panel: the master, which sets up and runs the whole site, and room panels, which give each space its scenes and brightness. This guide walks through the screens in the order you'll meet them on a job.

1 · In the office

Venlux Setup

A desktop app for the intake workbook: one Excel file that describes the site. Fill it in from the drawings, check it, then deploy it to the processors. The workbook still opens in Excel.

2 · Running the site

The master panel

The master runs on a TSW panel, the iPad (Crestron One) or a laptop browser (WebXPanel). It sees every area; what a person may change depends on their level (User, Technician, Administrator).

3 · Commissioning

Setting up a site

The site arrives from the intake workbook (built in Venlux Setup). Everything on site is set on the master under Setup, saved on the primary processor, and kept over later workbook imports.

4 · Other control systems

Integrations

An AV or building system can recall scenes and presets, set levels, combine rooms and follow the lighting over plain TCP text (port 8094 on the primary). Each integration has its own key, access and scope.

5 · Hardware

Devices and panels

The device plan comes from the intake workbook; on site you find the real devices, match them to the plan and record what Toolbox set. Venlux never changes device settings itself.

6 · In each space

Room panels

Room panels (designed for the 7" TSW-770, 1280 × 800) show only the areas assigned to them, in the simplest form: scenes, one brightness control, and divide or combine.

The demo site's names and logo are made up; a real site shows its own.
